# Character screen scene (client)

The character screen stands in the world, like tgiann-multichar's set view (its "city" set is the default scene: a Vinewood hills terrace at 20:56, five spots).

- Panel data: `characterScene` in ai-core `data/settings.json` (Character screen tab): field of view, optional own time and weather, and up to 10 spots. A spot is the ped position (root coordinates, as `GetEntityCoords` reads them), an idle anim, the camera (distance, height slope, angle around the ped, look-at height, sway in degrees) and optional depth-of-field blur. Values are tgiann-multichar's set slots renamed: `coordHeight` = look point height, `heading` = camera angle, `leftRightValue` = sway.
- The scene arrives with the character screen snapshot (`CharacterScreen.scene`); nothing else is sent.
- Client: the player waits invisible and frozen 5 m under the focused spot (collision and map stream there; ai-appearance renders its portrait peds there, out of the camera's view). Each character is a local ped (`CreatePed(..., false, false)`) on the spot of its slot; a character in a slot past the spots stands on spot `(slot - 1) % count` only while it is picked. Picking a card (`focus` NUI callback) glides the camera there (new camera, `SetCamActiveWithInterp` 500 ms); the camera sways by `SetCamParams` legs of 10 s and shakes lightly (`HAND_SHAKE` 0.15).
- Camera position: look point = ped root + look height; camera = look point + distance in the direction of heading `ped heading + angle`, raised by `atan(height slope)`.
- Looks: ai-appearance keeps the card looks it receives for the portraits and announces each with the client event `ai-core:client:characterLook (charId)`; ai-core then calls its client export `ApplyCharacterAppearance(ped, charId)` (also right after creating a ped, for looks that came first). Without ai-appearance the peds keep the default freemode clothes.
- Time and weather: `NetworkOverrideClockTime` + `SetOverrideWeather`, with the weather sync paused through the `qb-weathersync:client:DisableSync` / `EnableSync` events (ai-weathersync answers them); leaving clears both overrides and resumes the sync.
- Leaving (play, resource stop, a failed build) deletes the peds, destroys the cameras, turns script cameras off, clears the focus area and the time / weather overrides. When the scene cannot be built the NUI shows `branding.background` instead (open payload `scene: false`).
